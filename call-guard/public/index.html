Remember on every call

  • No one needs your OTPNot your bank, not the police, not delivery. OTP किसी को न बताएं।
  • "Digital arrest" does not existPolice never arrest or question you on a video call or ask for money to clear your name.
  • Secrecy is the scam"Don't tell anyone, don't cut the call" is what fraudsters say. Hang up and tell family.
  • Never move money to a "safe account"RBI and government agencies do not hold your money for verification.
  • No screen-sharing appsNever install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or any app a caller asks for.
  • Call back yourselfHang up, then call the number printed on your card or the official website.
Lost money or shared details? Call the Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 Report online: cybercrime.gov.in · Report the number: sancharsaathi.gov.in (Chakshu)
Ruko Call Guard is a prototype. Audio is sent to Sarvam only to turn speech into text; long numbers are hidden before text is sent to OpenAI. Nothing is saved. It can miss scams or raise false alarms, so trust the rules above too.
